# Evaluation

Everything here was measured on **synthetic data**, on **one developer laptop**, by the developer. There was no usability study with clinical secretaries. The numbers show whether the method works on a realistic, adversarial drill. They say nothing about how it performs in a hospital. The dated raw log, including runs that went wrong, is [evaluation/gate-a-2026-11-21.md](evaluation/gate-a-2026-11-21.md).

## Setup

- **Machine:** laptop with 16 GB RAM and an RTX 3060; Chrome 158; Gemini Nano `available`; local context window of 9,216 tokens.
- **App:** the hosted build (`https://lantern-downtime.web.app`), Lab page `#/lab`.
- **Cloud:** Firebase AI Logic with the Gemini Developer API (free tier at the time of these runs), App Check enforced, and the models set in Remote Config.

## Data

**Development set** (`public/fixtures/dev/`, key in `dev-key.json`). Three improvised layouts of 10 rows each, with different headers, one of them a single free-text column. It was used for tuning prompts and rules and is kept separate from the benchmark.

**Frozen benchmark: the Ward 7B drill.** It is generated by `scripts/build-drill.mjs`; the answer key in `public/fixtures/drill/answer-key.json` is never shown to any model. The story is a night shift with the electronic record down from 21:40 to 08:30:

- **Census:** 20 beds, printed at 21:00 (bed 6 empty).
- **Version 1** (emailed at 03:10): 24 rows, 6 columns.
- **Version 2** (a nurse's copy, replied-all at 07:40): 26 rows. She renamed "Time" to "Time given", added a "Comments" column, and sorted by bed, which lost 3 rows of version 1 (a fall, a neuro observation and a family phone call).

The 26 rows of version 2:

| Kind | Rows | Traps |
|---|---|---|
| Unchanged copies | 12 | none: the exact-match baseline should get these |
| Re-typed copies (paraphrases) | 6 | "PCM 1g IV" vs "Paracetamol 1g IV given"; "O2 2L via nasal specs" vs "O2 2L NP started"; a missing time; the name variant "Anna Ribeiro"; a transposed hospital number (H-209921 for H-209912) with the drug typo "ceftriaxon" |
| Documented corrections | 3 | a time (02:00 → 02:30), a dose (oxycodone 5 mg → 10 mg), a status (enoxaparin "NOT given, pt refused"), each with a comment in the sheet |
| New acts | 3 | a second PRN morphine dose 3 h 15 after the first and a later glucose check (both must not be merged); an admission not on the census |
| Ambiguous | 2 | "MS 10mg given" (morphine or magnesium sulfate?); a row identified by bed only, in a bed that changed patient during the night |

**Expected outcome:**
- 21 same-act pairs (12 copies, 6 re-typings, 3 corrections) and 3 pairs that must not be merged;
- 3 amendments, 2 open questions with an owner, and 4 identity issues (name variant, ID mismatch, not in the census, no identity);
- **27 acts ready for re-entry and 2 unresolved.**

## Methods compared

| Method | What it does |
|---|---|
| Latest file only | Keeps version 2 as is, which is what a hurried re-entry often does |
| Exact copies only | Merges rows identical in patient, time and text, and keeps everything else |
| Lantern | The full pipeline: adaptive mapping, identity, exact copies, an on-device "same act?" judgment (cloud fallback without Gemini Nano), deterministic guard |
| Lantern + second opinion | The same, plus a cloud second opinion on escalated pairs |

The Lab labels the third method "on-device" even when it ran without Gemini Nano. The pair-judgment line of each run says where the calls actually ran.

## Metrics (`src/core/score.ts`)

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Same-act recall | Gold same-act pairs that Lantern links (the later row proposed as a duplicate of the earlier one), out of 21 |
| Precision | Correct links out of all proposed duplicates |
| False merges | Must-not-merge pairs that were linked |
| Corrections found | Gold amendments proposed as `corrected` and linked to the right row, out of 3 |
| False corrections | Rows proposed as `corrected` with no documented correction |
| Open questions kept | Ambiguous rows left `unresolved`, out of 2 |
| v1-only kept | The 3 rows missing from version 2 that still reach the worklist |
| Identity | Gold identity issues detected, out of 4 |
| Ready / open | Rows approved or corrected (expected 27) and rows unresolved (expected 2) |
| Acts lost / entered twice | Real acts with no row in the worklist, or with more than one |

## Results

### Column mapping and extraction (development set, Gemini Nano, Lab S3/S3m)

| Strategy | Correct columns | Time (3 sheets) | Flagged for review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole table, raw | 15/19 | 17.7 s | — |
| Whole table + value-shape reconciliation | **19/19** | 17.7 s | 4 |
| One prompt per column, raw | 19/19 | 34.3 s | 0 |
| One prompt per column + reconciliation | 19/19 | 34.3 s | 0 |

- **Mapping:** the adaptive strategy (whole table, then per-column prompts only for disputed columns) mapped every drill column correctly in all runs: 3/3, 6/6 and 7/7.
- **Extraction** (Lab only, not used by the product): 149/154 fields correct (97%), median warm latency 3.2 s per entry. Two invented values were caught by the verbatim-source check.

### What the rules and the second opinion contributed

- **Nano misses a genuine repeat dose.** In every run, Nano called the second PRN morphine dose (01:45, 3 h 15 after the first) the same act. The time rule separated it every time.
- **Corrections come back as "same act".** Nano and the cloud answered the three documented corrections with "same act". The correction rule promoted them to amendments, using the comment written in the sheet. The prompt asks for exactly this.
- **The second opinion catches local errors.** In run 3, 7 calls fixed 4 local errors, and the one raw answer that contradicted a correct local answer was overruled by the same rules. In the cloud-only run, one raw second-opinion answer was also overruled. Every verdict, local or cloud, goes through the same guard.

### A failure mode found and fixed: on-device stalls

- **The stall.** In runs 1 and 2, one on-device call (a different pair each time) ran for about 6 minutes and returned nothing usable. This accounts for the extra ~345 s. The same happened once in an earlier run with a four-way relation prompt (372 s).
- **Likely cause.** It is consistent with constrained decoding looping inside the unbounded free-text `evidence` field until the context window is full.
- **What happened to the pair.** It was not lost: it went to the reviewer as "possibly typed twice" with no suggestion, which is why "ready" reads 28.
- **The fix.** Prompt v3 limits the evidence to 12 words. Every on-device call now has a 45 s deadline (`AbortSignal` through `promptOptions`) and one retry on a fresh session.
- **The result.** Runs 4 and 5 had no stall and took about 21 s.

### Cloud models on the free tier

AI Logic rejects calls without an App Check token (`401`). Lab S4 passed from the hosted origin in desktop Chrome: `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` answered in 9.5 s in Chrome and in 1.7–1.9 s in repeated headless runs. A probe with one tiny prompt per model on the free tier gave:

| Model | Result |
|---|---|
| `gemini-3.8-flash` | 500 "high demand", then 429 quota exceeded |
| `gemini-3.5-flash` (served as `gemini-3.6-flash`) | OK, 4.9–42.7 s |
| `gemini-3.6-flash` | OK, 68.0 s |
| `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` | OK, 0.7–1.9 s |
| `gemini-4-hydrogen` | 503 after 179 s |

So Remote Config sets `cloud_model = gemini-3.5-flash-lite` and `cloud_fallback_model = gemini-3.6-flash`, and either can be changed without a new build.

### End-to-end test on 2026-11-28

The hosted build `df2bda1`, on the developer's Windows laptop (Chrome 158 with Gemini Nano), Firefox and a phone:

| Part | Setting | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A | Chrome with Gemini Nano | pass. The columns were read and compared in about 20 s, with 14 on-device calls and 0 cloud. The review survived a reload with its decisions and counters. The worklist had 27 acts and 2 questions, and printed one page per person. Every drill check was green. `#/check` was green, and its local test made 0 requests to Google. |
| B | Firefox, no on-device AI | pass. The comparison ran in the cloud (11 fictional rows, 0 on-device). The second opinion took this to 22 rows; on the time correction it agreed with Lantern's rules, against the first cloud reading. |
| C | Firefox, strict tracking protection | Google's reCAPTCHA was blocked, so App Check refused the cloud. Lantern fell back to its rules within seconds and sent nothing, but it did not say why. Fixed: see below. |
| D | Xiaomi 14, Chrome | pass. No horizontal scroll; the bar and buttons were usable. |
| E | Chrome restarted with Wi-Fi off | pass (S1 above). |

The test found three problems, and all three are fixed:
- **"(on-device)" on a cloud answer.** Without Gemini Nano, the reason under a "same act" suggestion said "(on-device)", although the reading above it correctly said the cloud. It now names where the opinion ran.
- **Two category labels.** "Urine output 30ml…" and "Pulled out NG tube…" were labelled Medication, because a volume and the letters "NG" looked like a dose and a route. They are now Observation and Nursing care. No other drill row changed category.
- **No explanation when the cloud was blocked.** When a browser has no on-device AI and the cloud cannot be reached, the review now says so at the top, explains the likely cause (a tracker blocker or the network blocking reCAPTCHA) and says how to see the AI. In that case it no longer offers a second opinion that would fail the same way.

## Limitations

- **Synthetic data only.** The drill was written by the developer to contain known traps. It is one ward and one night, in English. Real logs will be messier.
- **One machine.** Gemini Nano latency and stability vary with hardware and Chrome version; the stall above was found on this laptop only.
- **No usability study.** No clinical secretary has used Lantern yet, so nothing here measures review time or errors made by people.
- **Small numbers.** 11 judged pairs per run and 5 runs cannot support statistical claims.
- **Free-tier measurements.** The cloud figures above were measured on the free tier: latency ranged from under 1 s to over a minute depending on the model and load, and rate limits (429) occurred. Since 2026-11-26 the hosted demo uses the paid tier; production use would still need its own capacity planning.
- **Tracker blockers.** Strict tracking protection (Firefox) and some blockers stop Google's reCAPTCHA, so App Check, and with it the cloud, is unavailable there. A browser without on-device AI then gets Lantern's rules only (see the end-to-end test).
- **Development history.** Prompts and rules were tuned on the development set and on drill runs, so the drill is not a blind test of the final version.

## Reproducing

- **In the browser:** open `#/lab` → S5 → Run. Tick "with cloud second opinion" for the cloud comparison. The page prints the per-pair table and the method comparison, and can download a JSON report.
- **Offline, no model:** `npm test` runs `tests/core/drill.test.ts`:
  - the identity checks, the 12 exact copies, and the expected worklist with a perfect judge;
  - the rules neutralising a careless judge;
  - a replay of a recorded on-device run;
  - the exact-copies-only baseline.
